## Gamer's perspective (qualitative, desktop walkthrough)

**What works:** The "Abhimanyu got in. Can you get out?" hook is excellent.
The immediate **Yuddh karo** button avoids sign-up friction. Temple-punk
brass/indigo visuals and Sanjaya give clear identity. Hidden ranks, turning
formations, day/night advantages and Shakti are a compelling combination;
the two-tap attack confirmation reduces costly thumb slips. The guided lesson
builds competence by showing one verb at a time.

**Friction / risks to validate:** Several unfamiliar terms (Makara, Padma,
Shakti, Durg and the astras) compete for working memory. Board glyphs and
rank badges need a **physical phone** readability test, especially in sunlight.
The Intel panel is dense for a first-timer. Full-screen combat beats may feel
rewarding once but too frequent in repeated matches; observe actual sessions
before changing their timing. The Daily challenge alone is not yet a durable
social loop without friend challenges/replays. No player retention data exists.
