## Title

VYUHA: Chakravyuha Strategy

## English short description

Read hidden ranks, wield astras, and break the Daily Chakravyuha.

## English full description

Abhimanyu got in. Can you get out?

The wheel is waiting. Eight raiders stand outside a guarded fort, the ranks ahead are hidden, and the rings will turn again. Your strongest warrior cannot answer every threat. A scout, an engineer, or one well-timed arrow can open the way.

VYUHA is a strategy game rooted in the formations and storytelling of the Indian epics. Command an army of hidden ranks, learn what the enemy reveals, and break the gate of their fort. Face the computer or share one device with a friend. You can begin as a guest and learn on the field.

READ THE ARMY

A piece that has never moved might be a trap. A warrior moving down a long lane tells you something about its role. Every clash adds another clue. Use Divyadrishti to reveal an enemy, inspect the intel ledger, and check the displayed odds before committing to an unknown target.

The army has different tools for different problems. A Rath can travel along an open lane. An Ashvarohi can dash. A Dhanurdhar can loose a volley at a target exactly two cells away. A Yantrik clears the trap that could stop a stronger warrior. The Chara is weak in an ordinary clash but dangerous when it strikes a Senapati or Maharathi first.

FIGHT ON A TURNING FIELD

The Chakravyuha is a wheel, with spokes, rings, and an exposed central hub. The Padmavyuha is a lotus field built from connected hexagons. Their inner rings turn as the battle advances, carrying pieces and revealing them. Two square fields offer different spaces to learn, plan, and defend. An open route may change when the field turns.

WAIT FOR YOUR HOUR

Surya's hour favours Suryavansh on the attack; Chandra's hour favours Chandravansh. A Makara pincer rewards a supported strike. A Padma guard strengthens a piece protected by its companions. You can read these bonuses before a clash. Choose whether to attack now, strengthen your position, or let the changing hour do part of the work.

LIGHT THE DIYAS

Captures earn Shakti, shown as five lamps. Spend it on wind, trance, fire, or light. Vayavyastra exchanges two friendly pieces. Sammohanastra holds an enemy still for two of its turns. Agneyastra burns lighter targets and opens a fort gate. Brahmastra can destroy a fighting piece and reveal its neighbours. Each astra is available once per battle, so a dramatic moment also asks a practical question: will you need it more later?

ONE DAILY CHALLENGE

The Daily Chakravyuha gives everyone the same seeded starting puzzle for the date in India. Eight raiders face ten defenders under a round limit, with a modifier that changes the day's conditions. Sundays use the lotus field. The first completed result is your official result; later runs are practice. Share a compact battle grid to WhatsApp or copy it wherever your friends gather. Compare decisions, then return for a different challenge tomorrow.

START WITH PEHLA YUDDH

The guided first battle introduces movement, sight, arrows, strikes, traps, and the fort. Sanjaya offers calm narration in Hinglish, Hindi, or English. When you are ready, face Sainik, Dronacharya, or Shakuni, or customise the field, rules, tie outcome, and turn clock.

FEATURES

- Hidden ranks, readable clues, and an intel ledger.
- Four battlefields, including the wheel and lotus.
- Fort gates, changing hours, formations, and four astras.
- Daily challenges, practice runs, streaks, and share cards.
- Three computer opponents and local pass-and-play.
- Local profiles, saved games, and backup export.
- Colour-blind colours, high contrast, and reduced motion options.

VYUHA presents warriors with dignity and astras as elements. No deity is a playing piece or a capture. There are no real-money mechanics. In the current browser edition, profiles and saves stay on your device; no account is required. After its files have loaded and been cached, the browser edition supports offline play. Keep a backup if you clear browser data or change devices.

Take the field. Read the silence. Find the opening.

## Hinglish full description

Abhimanyu got in. Can you get out?

Saamne chakravyuha hai. Tumhare aath yoddha ek surakshit durg ki taraf badh rahe hain. Dushman ke ranks chhupe hain, aur kuch rounds baad andar ke ghere phir ghoomenge. Sirf sabse taakatvar yoddha aage bhejna kaafi nahi. Kabhi ek sahi nazar, kabhi ek Yantrik, aur kabhi waqt par chhoda gaya teer raasta kholta hai.

VYUHA, Bharatiya mahaakaavyon ki sena rachnaon aur kahani sunane ki parampara se prerit strategy game hai. Apni chhupi hui sena sambhalo, dushman ki chaalon se jaankari jodo, aur uske durg ka dwaar todo. Computer ke khilaaf khelo ya ek hi device par dost ke saath baari badlo. Account zaroori nahi.

HAR CHAAL EK ISHAARA HAI

Jo mohra ab tak hila nahi, woh jaal bhi ho sakta hai. Divyadrishti se chhupa mohra dekho, intel panel mein bachi hui sena samjho, aur anjaan dushman par vaar karne se pehle jeet ke chances dekho.

Har yoddha ka apna kaam hai. Rath khule raaste par door jaata hai. Ashvarohi tez badhta hai. Dhanurdhar theek do cells door teer chala sakta hai. Yantrik us jaal ko hataata hai jahan bada yoddha bhi haar sakta hai. Chara aam takkar mein kamzor hai, lekin pehla vaar kare toh Senapati ya Maharathi ko bhi gira sakta hai. Sahi samay par sahi mohra kaam aata hai.

MAIDAAN BHI CHAAL CHALTA HAI

Chakravyuha mein ghere, seedhe raaste aur ek khula beech ka sthaan hai. Padmavyuha ka maidaan kamal jaisa hai, jude hue chhah-kone cells ke saath. Dono ke andar ke ghere waqt aane par ghoomte hain, mohron ko saath le jaate hain, aur unki pehchaan kholte hain. Do chaukore maidaan bhi hain.

APNE PRAHAR KA INTEZAAR KARO

Surya ke prahar mein Suryavansh ko hamle ka faayda milta hai. Chandra ke prahar mein Chandravansh ka samay aata hai. Makara ghera ek saathi ke sahaare kiye gaye vaar ko mazboot karta hai. Padma raksha mein paas khade saathi apne yoddha ko sambhalte hain. Takkar se pehle yeh faayde dikhte hain. Abhi hamla karna hai, jagah behtar karni hai, ya prahar badalne dena hai: faisla tumhara hai.

SHAKTI KE DIYE JALAO

Dushman ka mohra lene par Shakti milti hai, jo paanch diyon mein dikhti hai. Vayavyastra se apne do mohron ki jagah badlo. Sammohanastra se ek dushman ko uski do baariyon tak rok do. Agneyastra se halke mohre aur jaal jalao, ya durg ka dwaar kholo. Brahmastra se ek ladne wala mohra hatao aur uske aas-paas ka raaz kholo. Har astra ek battle mein ek hi baar milta hai.

ROZ EK NAYA CHALLENGE

Daily Chakravyuha mein India ki tareekh ke hisaab se sabko ek hi seeded shuruaat milti hai. Aath hamlaavar, das rakshak, aur rounds ki seema. Din ka modifier shuruaati haalaat badalta hai. Sunday ko kamal wala maidaan aata hai. Pehla poora kiya gaya result official rehta hai; baad ke runs practice hote hain. Apni battle ka chhota grid WhatsApp par bhejo ya copy karke doston ke saath baanto. Agle din phir ek nayi paheli milegi.

PEHLA YUDDH SE SHURU KARO

Guided battle mein chalna, dekhna, teer chalaana, vaar karna, jaal hataana aur durg lena seekho. Sanjaya Hinglish, Hindi ya English mein shaant andaaz se saath deta hai. Sainik, Dronacharya ya Shakuni se lado. Custom battle mein maidaan, rules, barabari ka nateeja aur turn clock bhi chun sakte ho.
