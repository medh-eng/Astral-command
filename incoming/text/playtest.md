# VYUHA — first-phone playtest

Task 6 · Allow 15–20 minutes. One friend, their own phone, Pehla Yuddh, then one Daily. Ask permission to take notes; recording is optional and needs separate agreement. Use initials only. Do not put personal contact details in the public repository.

**Say before starting:** “Please try this game as you normally would. We're testing the game, and anything confusing helps us improve it. You can stop whenever you like. If you're comfortable, say what you're thinking. I'll mostly stay quiet. Start wherever makes sense to you.”

**Do not say:** which button to press, that it is easy, what a symbol means, or what you want them to like. Avoid “Did you notice…?” and “Don't you think…?” If stuck, ask “What would you try next?” Record any help you give. After two minutes stuck, offer a hint and mark the task as assisted.

**Watch silently:**

1. Where their first tap goes; whether Pehla Yuddh is discoverable.
2. Whether they know whose turn it is and which pieces belong to them.
3. How they understand hidden ranks and a piece becoming revealed.
4. Whether selecting, moving and confirming a strike are clear; repeated or missed taps.
5. How they discover sight, volley and the engineer's trap role.
6. Whether the fort gate, moving rings and day/night changes make sense.
7. Whether Shakti, astra cost and a spent astra are understandable on the phone.
8. What they do after a result: share, practise, leave, or look for another match.

**Ask after:**

1. “In your own words, what were you trying to do?”
2. “What did you think was happening when that piece changed?”
3. “Where did you feel unsure about your next action?”
4. “What made you choose your most memorable move?”
5. “What would you change about what you saw or heard?”
6. “What, if anything, would make you open this again?”
